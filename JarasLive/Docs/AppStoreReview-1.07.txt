EXPLICAÇÃO PARA A APPLE — RASCUNHO EM INGLÊS
Hello App Review,

We would like to clarify the purchase model for CatLive Native on iPad.
Native mode uses a one-time, non-consumable purchase, not an auto-renewable
subscription. Remote mode remains free on iPad and iPhone.

The permanent unlock is com.hookdeveloper.jaraslive.ipad.native.
The separate free non-consumable product
com.hookdeveloper.jaraslive.ipad.native.trial7days provides the "7-day Trial".
This follows the time-based trial model for non-subscription apps described
in Guideline 3.1.1. It does not renew or charge automatically.

Before starting the trial, the app explains its duration, the Native features
that require purchase after expiry, and the localized price of the permanent
unlock. Trial expiry preserves projects and access to free Remote mode.
The trial is timed from the original verified Apple transaction, so restoring
purchases does not restart the seven days.
